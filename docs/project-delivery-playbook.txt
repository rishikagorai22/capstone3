Project Delivery Playbook
Kickoff: every project holds a client kickoff meeting within five business days of contract signature. The project manager shares a delivery plan, a staffing plan, and a communication plan at kickoff. Status reporting: project managers send a written status report to the client every week, covering progress, risks, and budget used. Budget control: if a project reaches 80 percent of its budget, the project manager must notify the Principal and agree a revised plan with the client. Putting a project on hold: when a client pauses a project, the project manager must notify the practice lead within two business days and document the reason for the hold. Consultants are released back to the staffing pool if the hold lasts longer than ten business days. Before a project restarts, the budget and timeline must be re-baselined and approved by the client. Closure: after completion, the team holds a lessons-learned session within 15 business days and archives all deliverables.
