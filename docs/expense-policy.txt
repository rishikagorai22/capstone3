Expense Reimbursement Policy
Consultants may claim reasonable business expenses incurred while delivering client work. All claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense, with itemized receipts attached. Client dinners are reimbursable up to 75 dollars per person, and alcohol is not reimbursable. Domestic flights must be booked in economy class at least 14 days in advance whenever possible. Hotel stays are reimbursable up to 250 dollars per night in major cities and 180 dollars per night elsewhere. Ground transport is reimbursed at cost, and rental cars require approval from the project manager. Claims over 1,000 dollars require approval from a Principal. Claims submitted after 60 days are not reimbursed except in documented exceptional circumstances. Reimbursements are paid within 10 business days of approval.
