Consultant Onboarding Process
All new consultants follow a four-week onboarding program. Before the start date, IT ships a laptop and sets up accounts. On day one, the consultant meets their onboarding buddy, completes security and compliance training, and receives access to the time-tracking system. During week one, new hires attend firm orientation and meet their practice lead. In weeks two and three, the consultant shadows an active client engagement and begins logging billable hours under supervision. In week four, the consultant takes on a first assigned task. Check-in meetings with the practice lead take place at 30, 60, and 90 days. Associates also complete a two-day analytics tooling workshop in their first month. Senior hires and Principals complete a one-day client-engagement leadership session instead. Every new consultant must complete the mandatory data-handling course before they are given access to any client data.
