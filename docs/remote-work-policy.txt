Remote Work Policy
Consultants may work remotely up to three days per week unless the client engagement requires on-site presence. Core collaboration hours are 10am to 3pm local time, during which consultants are expected to be available. New hires must work on-site for their first two weeks. Each employee receives a one-time home office stipend of 600 dollars to purchase a desk, chair, monitor, or similar equipment, and receipts must be submitted within 60 days of the employee's start date. Internet costs are not reimbursed. Employees must use the firm VPN when accessing client data from home, and client data may never be stored on personal devices. Working remotely from another country requires written approval from HR at least 30 days in advance because of tax and legal implications.
